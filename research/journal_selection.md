## Selected journal: **Journal of Complex Networks** (Oxford University Press)

- Homepage: `https://academic.oup.com/comnet` — verified reachable and
  titled "Journal of Complex Networks | Oxford Academic".
- **Scope (verified)**, from `https://academic.oup.com/comnet/pages/about`
  (retrieved 2026-10-18), quoted verbatim:
  > "Journal of Complex Networks publishes original articles and reviews with a
  > significant contribution to the analysis and understanding of complex networks and
  > its applications in diverse fields."

  and topic list includes, verbatim:
  > "Mathematical and numerical analysis of networks"
  > "Network theory and computer sciences"
  > "Structural analysis of networks"

  Fit: the paper computes the exact local structure (fixed-subgraph densities,
  triangles/C4/stars, second moments) of a random network model used across the
  network-science literature; this sits squarely in "Mathematical and numerical
  analysis of networks" and "Structural analysis of networks".
- **No-mandatory-fee route (verified)**, from
  `https://academic.oup.com/comnet/pages/General_Instructions` (retrieved 2026-10-18),
  quoted verbatim:
  > "Journal of Complex Networks offers the option of publishing under either a
  > standard licence or an open access licence."

  > "Should you wish to publish your article open access, you should select your choice
  > of open access licence in our online system after your article has been accepted for
  > publication. You will need to pay an open access charge to publish under an open
  > access licence."

  Reading: the charge is attached to *choosing* the open-access licence; the standard
  licence (subscription/hybrid) route has **no mandatory author fee** stated anywhere on
  the author-instructions page. No "submission fee" or "page charge" appears on the page.
- **Preprint / repository policy (verified)**, same page, "Preprints" section
  (retrieved 2026-10-18), quoted verbatim:
  > "As an author, you retain the right to make an Author's Original Version (preprint)
  > available through various channels and this does not prevent submission to the
  > Journal. If accepted, the authors are required to update the status of any preprint,
  > including adding your published paper's DOI."

  Reading: arXiv/GitHub preprint dissemination of the Author's Original Version is
  allowed; **the public repository with the preprint is compatible with submission**.
  The published version of the paper (once published) must not be additionally mirrored
  without the journal's terms.
- Journal ident facts (verified from the about page, retrieved 2026-10-18):
  online-only, published online ISSN 2051-1329, six issues/year, 2025 Impact Factor 1.9,
  indexed in Scopus, Mathematical Reviews and Zentralblatt Math — relevant for a
  combinatorial-probability audience.
- Data/code policy (verified, same instructions page, retrieved 2026-10-18), quoted
  verbatim: "Where ethically feasible, Journal of Complex Networks strongly encourages
  authors to make all data and software code on which the conclusions of the paper rely
  available to readers." — matches our public-repo plan.

fee, verified 2026-10-18), with the preprint claim disclosed as per the verified
preprint policy. Dissemination-wise: GitHub public repo = Author's Original Version
materials + code; compatible with the journal's preprint statement quoted above.
